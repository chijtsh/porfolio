Forests

Tropical

Tropical forests are warm, humid environments filled with diverse plants and wildlife. They receive plenty of rainfall throughout the year.

Boreal

Boreal forests are cold regions dominated by evergreen trees such as pine, spruce, and fir. They are commonly found in northern parts of the world.

Temperate

Temperate forests experience distinct seasons and are home to a variety of trees and animals. Many of their trees lose their leaves during autumn.

Mountains

Mount Everest

Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level, reaching about 8,849 meters. It is located in the Himalayas along the Nepal–China border.

Mount Fuji

Mount Fuji is Japan's tallest mountain and a well-known cultural symbol. Its symmetrical shape and snow-covered peak attract visitors from around the world.

Mount Kilimanjaro

Mount Kilimanjaro is Africa's highest mountain, located in Tanzania. It is famous for its snow-capped peak rising above the surrounding grasslands.

Oceans

Pacific

The Pacific Ocean is the largest and deepest ocean on Earth. It stretches between Asia, Australia, and the Americas.

Atlantic

The Atlantic Ocean separates the Americas from Europe and Africa. It is an important route for international trade and marine life.

Indian

The Indian Ocean is the third-largest ocean and is surrounded by Africa, Asia, and Australia. It is known for its warm waters and diverse marine ecosystems.

Wildlife

Giant Panda

Giant pandas are bears native to China and are well known for their black-and-white fur. They mainly feed on bamboo and spend much of their day eating.

Lion

Lions are large social cats commonly found in Africa. They live in groups called prides and are known for their strength and distinctive manes.

African Elephant

African elephants are the largest land animals on Earth. They are highly intelligent animals known for their large ears, trunks, and strong family groups.

About GreenNest

GreenNest is a nature and environment showcase created to celebrate the beauty and diversity of our planet. The website features forests, mountains, oceans, and wildlife through a collection of photographs and short descriptions. Our goal is to encourage people to appreciate nature and become more aware of the importance of protecting our environment. Through GreenNest, we hope to inspire simple actions that can help preserve our planet for future generations.
